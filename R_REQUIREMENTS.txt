Mortgage Credit Risk Portfolio
R Environment Requirements

R version: 4.6.1

Required package:
cmprsk==2.2-12

Purpose:
The cmprsk package is used in scripts/15_fine_gray_default_risk.R
to estimate the Fine-Gray subdistribution hazard model for Default,
with Voluntary Prepayment treated as a competing event.

Note:
The Fine-Gray model is estimated on the full survival cohort.
The current implementation reports point estimates only
(subdistribution hazard ratios); variance estimation is disabled.
